⭐ Introduction Page Description

WebGuard is an all-in-one digital safety app designed to protect users from online threats.
It helps identify fake news, scam websites, fraud credit cards, and suspicious messages.
With simple tools, a friendly chatbot, and helpful videos, WebGuard makes online safety easy for everyone.

🤖 Chatbot Page

The WebGuard Chatbot acts like your personal digital safety assistant.
You can ask anything related to online fraud, fake messages, cyber safety tips, or verification help.
The chatbot replies instantly with clear, simple answers.

Features:
✔ Answers questions about fraud
✔ Helps detect fake news or websites
✔ Guides users on safe internet practices
✔ Easy and user-friendly conversation

🎥 Videos Page

The Videos section provides short, useful clips that teach users:

How to avoid online scams

How fake news spreads

How scam websites look

Cyber safety tips for students and working professionals

These videos make learning simple and visual.

📰 Fake News Detection

This feature checks whether a news statement or headline is real or fake.
You simply enter the news text, and the model analyzes patterns and compares it with trusted information to classify it as:

Real News

Fake News

This protects users from misinformation and rumor spreading.

🌍 Fake Website Detection

This tool scans a website link (URL) and checks:

Website reputation

SSL certificate

Phishing patterns

Suspicious keywords

Unsafe redirects

If the site looks harmful, WebGuard warns the user immediately:

⚠️ “This website may be fake or unsafe.”

If safe:
✔️ “This website appears real and secure.”

💳 Fake Credit Card Detection

The app can detect whether a credit card transaction or number looks genuine or fraudulent using:

Machine learning (V-features detection)

Anomaly detection

Pattern matching

Users can enter details (like V28 in your project), and the app returns:

Normal Transaction
or

Fraudulent Transaction

This helps prevent financial scams.

🛡️ Overall Vision

WebGuard aims to make the internet a safer place.
Whether it's verifying news, checking a website, scanning a credit card, or asking a question—the app protects users from digital fraud in a simple, easy way.
